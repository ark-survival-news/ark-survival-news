A Message From Studio Wildcard

After careful deliberation and a comprehensive internal review of our infrastructure roadmap, we are writing today to inform our community of a significant operational change affecting both ARK: Survival Evolved and ARK: Survival Ascended official server networks globally.

Effective June 15, 2026 at 11:59 PM UTC, all Studio Wildcard-operated official servers for both titles will be taken offline permanently. This includes all PvP, PvE, Small Tribes, and Hardcore clusters across all regions for both games.

This decision was not made lightly. The ARK community has been an extraordinary part of our journey since Early Access, and we understand this news carries real weight for players who have invested thousands of hours into their tribes and builds. We want to be as transparent as possible about what is happening and why.

What Is Changing

Studio Wildcard will be consolidating its server operations as part of a broader infrastructure partnership arrangement. Going forward, official game servers for both ARK: Survival Evolved and ARK: Survival Ascended will no longer be operated or maintained directly by Studio Wildcard. Unofficial and player-hosted dedicated servers are not affected by this change and will continue to function normally.

!

Character and tribe data will not be transferable between official and unofficial servers. We strongly encourage all players to back up their single-player saves and explore unofficial hosting options before the shutdown window.

Timeline

Shutdown Schedule — All Times UTC

June 15, 2026 — 11:59 PM UTC All official servers permanently taken offline.

Your Options Going Forward

Unofficial servers hosted by community members and third-party providers will remain fully operational. Many high-quality unofficial clusters already exist with active populations, including boosted rates and QoL mods that the official network did not support.

Players looking to continue their ARK experience are encouraged to visit the community server browser and ARK’s subreddit and Discord, where server owners are already organizing migration events and offering tribe transfer assistance.

We recognize this is a disruptive change on short notice. We are committed to supporting the transition in whatever way we are able over the coming weeks.

Thank You

To every tribe, every tamer, every survivor who has spent nights on the Island, on Aberration, in Genesis — this game exists because of you. The community you built will outlast any server.

With gratitude,

The Studio Wildcard Team
